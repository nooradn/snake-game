# Snake Game Mechanics

## Grid & World
- Grid width: 20 cells (estimated)
- Grid height: 20 cells (estimated)
- Cell size: 30 pixels (estimated, window 600x650 with HUD)

## Movement & Timing
- Move interval / Tick rate: 0.12 seconds per step (estimated)
- Controls: Arrow keys / WASD (estimated)

## Rules & Scoring
- Snake starts with length 3 (estimated)
- Food spawns at random unoccupied position (estimated)
- Eating food increases score by 10 and increases length by 1 (estimated)
- Hitting wall wraps or dies (dies / game over) (estimated)
- Hitting own tail ends game (game over) (estimated)
- High score tracking (estimated)

## Audio & Sounds
- Eat food sound: Procedural bright chiptune chirp / ascending tone (estimated)
- Turn / move tick sound: Subtle soft blip on steering direction changes (estimated)
- Game over sound: Low descending buzzing crunch (estimated)

## HUD
- Top bar displays current score and game state (estimated)
